Why Refka exists

It started on a gym floor, surrounded by strangers I saw every day.

A 180kg deadlift PR nearly ended with our founder on the floor — dizzy, alone, holding onto a rack, in a room full of people he recognized but didn't actually know. The question that followed wouldn't go away:

Why, in a room full of people training on the same schedule toward the same goals, was I completely alone?

He started noticing it everywhere. People training solo — same time slot, same routine, day after day — until some simply stopped showing up. Not because the gym failed them. Proximity was never the problem. Nobody had built the bridge between people already standing a few meters apart.

That question became Refka.

Refka — from an old word for "companionship." Two people, one loop, no end point.
The thesis

Churn isn't a motivation problem. It isn't a facilities problem. It's a belonging problem.

Gyms have solved equipment, programming, and pricing. Nobody has solved churn at the community layer. Members who train with someone show up for someone — and members who are expected don't quietly disappear.

Solo is the default

Most members walk in alone, train alone, and leave alone — even when compatible partners train meters away on the same schedule.

Solo quietly churns

Nobody notices when a solo member stops coming — least of all the member. Without a person expecting them, there's nothing to come back to.

Belonging retains

A training partner turns attendance into a shared commitment. Refka builds that connection — deliberately, for every member who wants one.

Pillar 1 — Retain

Your gym, with a community engine inside.

Refka is B2B SaaS: your gym subscribes, your members experience it as a native part of your gym — your brand, your floor, your community. Not another app they have to adopt. Retention is the foundation everything else is built on.

Step 1 — Match

Compatible partners, not random pairings

Members join through a simple QR intake at your reception. Refka matches them on schedule, goals, and experience level — the three things that make a partnership actually hold.

Step 2 — Meet

From match to first session

Refka moves matches to the floor: a first shared session, on a time both members already train. No awkward cold intros — they were always in the same room.

Step 3 — Stay

Shared commitment compounds

Partners keep each other accountable. You see the result where it matters: members who keep showing up, month after month — and a community your competitors can't copy.

Built to work with your team, not around it. Trainers stay central — Refka handles the social layer between members, so your staff can focus on coaching.
Pillar 2 — Earn

A community that spends together, inside your walls.

Retained members don't just renew — they buy. Refka opens a partner ecosystem around your gym and routes it through the member experience, so every recommendation earns for you: your gym takes a share of every partner transaction.

For investors — and for skeptics

We're proving it before we scale it.

Belonging is a nice word. Retention is a number. We track one funnel across our pilot gyms — and we don't scale the product or the narrative until it moves.

1
Match-to-meetup rate

Does the match convert to action?

A match that never trains together is a contact card. We measure how many matched pairs actually meet on the floor.

2
Repeat training rate

Does it stick?

One shared session is a moment. We measure whether pairs come back and train together again — the habit, not the event.

3
Retention lift

Does it move the number that matters?

The retention delta between matched and unmatched members. This is the metric gym owners pay for — and the proof the thesis stands on.

Built by a founder with 6+ years of retention and growth product management at Amazon, Uber, and Enpal — applied to an industry that has never solved churn at the community layer.
