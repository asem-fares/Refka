Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026  ·  Version 1.1  ·  Global scope

1. Who We Are

Refka ("we," "us," or "our") is a B2B2C fitness technology platform that provides gym management, member engagement, and AI-powered personalization services. Refka is the Data Controller for gym owner accounts, trainer accounts, brand partner accounts and website visitors. For gym member data, the gym owner is the Data Controller and Refka acts as the Data Processor on the gym owner's instructions, as set out in the Data Processing Addendum (Section 18 of our Terms of Service).

2. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to: Gym Owners (B2B customers who pay for and manage the platform), Gym Members (end users who join a gym via Refka), Personal Trainers (gym staff invited by owners), Brand Partners (marketplace partners), and Website Visitors.

3. Data We Collect

Gym Owners: name, phone number, gym name, city, country, address, GPS coordinates, working hours, logo, equipment inventory, membership plan details, and billing information for your Refka subscription.

Gym Members: first name, phone number, fitness goal, gender, training days, fitness level, and, with your explicit consent, body composition data (weight, height, body fat percentage, muscle mass from InBody or manual entry). We also store your AI-generated fitness and nutrition plans, training partner match preference, active status (whether you are currently at your gym), and attendance history.

Personal Trainers: name, phone, specializations, bio, photo, and the list of members assigned to you.

Body composition data is sensitive personal data. We collect it only with your explicit, separate consent and use it exclusively to generate your personalized fitness plan. It is never shared with advertisers or brand partners in identifiable form.

5. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to: create and manage your account; deliver and improve the Refka platform; generate AI-powered personalized fitness and nutrition plans; match you with a training partner at your gym (if you opt in); send service notifications via WhatsApp; process payments for gym subscriptions; and for platform security and fraud prevention.

6. AI Processing

When a fitness plan is generated for you, Refka sends an anonymized data packet to an AI model (currently Anthropic Claude or OpenAI). This packet never includes your name or phone number, only fitness-relevant data: body composition, goal, fitness level, training days, and your gym's equipment list. AI providers do not retain or train on this data after generating your plan. They are bound by Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) with Refka.

8. Data Sharing

Within the platform: gym owners see all data for their gym only. Trainers see only their assigned members. Members see their own data and their matched partner's first name and goal only. Brand partners never see individual member data, only anonymized, aggregated audience insights.

We never sell personal data. We never share health data with advertisers. We never give one gym access to another gym's data.

9. International Data Transfers

Data may be transferred to the United States or other countries where our sub-processors operate. For EU/EEA/Albanian residents, transfers are protected by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) under GDPR Art. 46(2)(c). For Saudi residents, transfers comply with PDPL Article 29 requirements.

10. Data Retention

Active account data is retained for the duration of your account. When you ask us to delete your account, personal data is soft-deleted immediately, then permanently purged within 30 days. Body composition data is deleted in full. Payment records are retained for 7 years (legal obligation). Equipment recognition photos are deleted within 24 hours of processing.

Gym owner business contact details (your name, your gym's name, phone, email and country) are kept after a free trial ends, a subscription is cancelled or an account is paused, so that we can stay in touch with you about Refka. We keep them on the basis of our legitimate interest in our business relationship with you. You can ask us to delete them at any time by writing to community@refka.tech, and every message we send tells you how to stop further messages. Your gym's member data is handled separately: it is deleted 90 days after your gym's account is paused, unless you reactivate it.

11. Your Rights

You have the right to: access a copy of your personal data; correct inaccurate data; erase your data (right to be forgotten); export your data in machine-readable format; withdraw consent for health data processing or partner communications at any time; restrict processing during a dispute.

EU/EEA/Albanian residents may also lodge a complaint with their national supervisory authority. Saudi residents may submit requests under PDPL. California residents may opt out of any sale of personal information (we conduct none).

To exercise your rights: community@refka.tech. We respond within 30 days.

The refka.tech website sets no cookies. It saves your language and country choice in your browser's local storage, and any visit statistics are collected without cookies. The Refka app uses only essential cookies or storage to keep you signed in and to protect your account. We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

13. Children

Refka is not directed at individuals under 16. If a gym registers a member under 16, the gym owner is responsible for obtaining valid parental consent. Contact community@refka.tech to report any inadvertent collection involving a minor.
